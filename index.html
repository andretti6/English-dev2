📝 Reading Practice

The World Cup is the most prestigious football tournament in the world. Every four years, national teams compete fiercely to win the famous trophy. While traditional giants like Brazil and France are always expected to be contenders, modern sports science and unpredictable strategies allow underdog teams to surprise everyone. Achieving victory requires tactical discipline and exceptional teamwork.

💡 Target Vocabulary

Prestigious: Highly respected / De prestígio.
Compete fiercely: To play with a lot of strength and passion.
Contenders: Teams with a real chance to win.
Underdog: A team that is not expected to win.

💬 Challenge Questions

  • Which team do you consider the biggest contender for 2026? Why?
  • Can you name a famous underdog team from past World Cups?
  • Write 3 sentences using the phrase "compete fiercely".
