💡 OBSIDIAN LESSON

Negotiation Tips in Practice

When you negotiate, listen to the other person first. Ask: "What do you need?" Next, show your ideas using clear numbers. Do not say your plan is the best. Just show how it saves time or money.

⚛️ ESSENTIALS CORNER

What is Zero Distance?

Zero Distance means two connected things react together at the exact same moment. It does not matter if they are millions of kilometers apart. Space is not a problem because they work as one team without any delay.
