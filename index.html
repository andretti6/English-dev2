How to develop Active Listening in real life:

To listen well, you don't just stay quiet. You must pay attention to the other person's energy. When they speak, do not think about your next answer. Instead, try to comprehend their perspective. A great trick is to paraphrase what you heard: say things like "So, what you mean is...?". This creates absolute clarity and builds incredible trust during a team project!
