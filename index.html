📝 Homework Task (Level A2)

     

        Write a short paragraph (50-70 words) about a famous singer or band from your country.         Imagine you want to create a museum for them. Where is the museum? Why will tourists love it?     

           

💡 Grammar Focus: Using "Because" to give strong reasons

     
        Example: Tourits will visit our city because the new music museum is very interactive and fun.
        Rule: Use 'because' to connect your project to a benefit!
